## Saskatchewan treatment intake

Confirmed 2026-08-02. Verify before repeating any number back to Jacob — these
change, and a wrong number when he has worked up to calling is a real cost.

**The one call: 306-655-7777** — Saskatchewan Health Authority mental health
and addictions central intake. This is the front door for detox, residential
treatment and outpatient. Self-referral; no doctor's note needed. Free.

Backups if that line is wrong or closed:
- **811** — HealthLine, 24/7, routes to addiction services.
- Regina Detox intake: 306-522-5662.
- Calder Centre, Saskatoon: 306-655-4500 ext. 4504.

**Free virtual program (no bed wait):** SHA funds 36 free virtual intensive
outpatient spaces through EHN Canada — 8 weeks of individual and group therapy
plus 10 months of aftercare. Adults 18+ anywhere in the province with a valid
SK health card. Enquiries are by form at
`edgewoodhealthnetwork.com/iopsa-sask/`, replied to within 2 business days, or
by phone at 1-866-963-6343. This matters because it does not depend on a
residential bed opening up.

Cost is not a barrier in any of the above. A valid SK health card is the only
requirement; if his has lapsed, replacing it becomes the blocking step.
