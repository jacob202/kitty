## Why this exists

Kitty accumulated 80 plan-shaped documents across five folders. Three separate
structures described the same delivery sequence under different names, and
finished plans never said they were finished — so each one kept presenting
itself as work owed, and agents repeatedly re-derived the order instead of
reading it.

On 2026-09-14 the live planning surface was reduced to two documents:

- [`docs/ACTIVE_MISSION.md`](../../ACTIVE_MISSION.md) — the one sequence, the
  real status of each step, and the evidence for that status. This is the
  control surface; agents update it rather than writing a new plan.
- [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md) — what "release-quality for Jacob"
  means. A definition of done, not a queue.
