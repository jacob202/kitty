DeepResearcher

Legacy technical research wrapper. Combines web search, scraping, synthesis, and optional knowledge promotion. General Research is being replaced by the engine-backed contract tracked in issue #547. Until then this module must remain honest about persistence: research results are not permanent knowledge unless promotion is explicitly requested and actually succeeds.
