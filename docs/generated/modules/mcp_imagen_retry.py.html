Overview

Tenacity-based retry decorator with exponential backoff. A single 429 from Gemini or DALL-E should not fail the whole call. With 3 attempts and exponential backoff (1-10s), transient rate-limit and service errors recover. Retryable exceptions are logged at WARNING with the engine name and attempt number — no payload dump (AGENTS.md prime directive).
