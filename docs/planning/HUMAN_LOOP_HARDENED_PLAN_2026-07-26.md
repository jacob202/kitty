# Kitty Human-Loop Hardened Plan

**Status:** Planning input for review; not active roadmap authority  
**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Source:** ChatGPT review of the Kitty founding document, the longer master document, `docs/NORTH_STAR.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, `docs/ACTIVE_MISSION.md`, and current repository evidence  
**Authority boundary:** `docs/ROADMAP.md` remains the only active roadmap. This document may be amended, partially adopted, or rejected during roadmap review.

## Decision this document proposes

Do not convert the founding insights into eight parallel product initiatives.

Convert them into one controlled product proof:

> Can Kitty carry one meaningful life obligation through interruption, preparation, action, verification, and re-entry while preserving Jacob's agency and telling the truth about what happened?

This keeps the frame large enough to hold the founding insight but narrow enough to execute.

## Why the first translation was not strong enough

The initial insight-to-action pass found real themes—continuity, lower initiation cost, cognitive offloading, evidence, adaptive support, agency, and Builder reliability—but translated them too quickly into a conventional feature portfolio.

That created two opposing errors:

1. **The frame became too small.** A discovery about capability, disability, continuity, agency, avoidance, and participation in life became a list of software subsystems.
2. **The scope became too large.** Nearly every insight became its own initiative before one complete human outcome loop had been proven.

The repository already contains the correction: life projects outrank Kitty itself, one trustworthy loop must finish before feature expansion, and infrastructure is justified only when it serves that loop.

## Evidence classification

### Verified

- Kitty's North Star is structure, memory, follow-through, and one concrete doable next move—not feature breadth.
- The active mission requires one real life-project resume loop before new feature lanes.
- KittyBuilder already contains substantial evidence, recovery, status, and failure-truthfulness machinery. A new generic "Evidence Engine" would duplicate an existing direction.
- Current roadmap authority explicitly forbids another queue, scheduler, state store, orchestrator, Builder cockpit, memory substrate, or broad feature lane before Phase 1 exits.

### Inference

- An eight-initiative program would become a competing roadmap and increase the risk that improving Kitty displaces the life outcome Kitty exists to support.
- The highest-leverage near-term proof is not predictive personalization. It is durable project state plus one action that survives interruption.

### Hypothesis

- State-aware support, adaptive task sizing, high-value interruption, and personal pattern modelling may become useful.
- They have not yet earned implementation and should remain experiments with explicit failure conditions.

## The stronger working definition

> Kitty is an adaptive continuity system that helps a person move from disrupted attention or AI conversation to one meaningful, verified real-world next action—without hiding uncertainty or removing agency.

This is a working product frame, not a permanent branding decision.

## The core Kitty loop

### 1. Notice

A commitment, insight, decision, deadline, problem, or unresolved question becomes visible through chat, deliberate capture, a project, email, calendar, or another supported source.

### 2. Preserve

Store the item with source, date, project, type, confidence, and whether it was directly stated or inferred. The user can correct or delete it. Inferences do not silently become obligations.

### 3. Orient

Reconstruct the truthful current state:

- what this is;
- why it matters;
- what has happened;
- what remains unresolved;
- what is blocked;
- what requires the user.

### 4. Select

Offer one primary next move. Allow a small set of meaningful alternatives: begin, prepare, deliberately reschedule, mark blocked, or dismiss as no longer relevant.

### 5. Prepare

Reduce initiation cost with bounded help: gather information, draft, locate a document, produce a checklist, prepare questions, or open the right place. Do not turn the action into another planning project.

### 6. Act

The user performs or explicitly authorizes consequential action. Low-risk internal organization may be automatic; external or consequential action remains permission-gated.

### 7. Verify

Record what actually happened: completed, submitted, sent, attended, rescheduled, blocked, abandoned, or partially completed. Distinguish inspectable evidence from self-report.

### 8. Resume

After interruption, return the project in a usable state and surface the next move without requiring chat archaeology.

The product proof is that the loop survives absence.

## Safety and assumption corrections

### Do not infer a capability score first

Start with an explicit user choice such as:

- I can plan.
- Give me one step.
- I am overwhelmed.
- Just preserve this for later.

Observable behaviour may adjust presentation, but Kitty should not quietly diagnose stress, sleep, activation, or capacity.

### Do not make support withdrawal the goal

Some scaffolding may fade. Some support may appropriately remain permanent. Success is preserved agency and reliable participation, not forced independence.

### Do not equate progress with shipping

Meaningful progress may be a call, meal, appointment, gathered document, deliberate reschedule, identified blocker, support contact, closed obligation, or code change. The distinction is not conversation versus shipping; it is exploration, preparation, action, verified change, deliberate postponement, and avoidance disguised as activity.

### Do not generalize prematurely

Jacob is the first deeply observed user, not proof of a universal disability or mental-health product. Prove the mechanism for him, test comprehension with another user later, and narrow claims to evidence.

## Hardened execution sequence

### Stage 1 — Classify the insights

Every extracted insight belongs in one of four buckets:

**Doctrine**

- life-first ordering;
- agency and meaningful consent;
- reversibility and correction;
- truthful uncertainty;
- graceful degradation;
- non-shaming interaction;
- evidence proportional to claims;
- human escalation where appropriate.

**Current-loop requirements**

- truthful project state;
- deliberate capture;
- one next action;
- bounded preparation;
- authorization;
- outcome receipt;
- resume after interruption.

**Research hypotheses**

- inferred functional state;
- high-value interruption;
- adaptive task sizing;
- trigger and timing analysis;
- crisis-sensitive behaviour;
- generalization to other users.

**Backlog capabilities**

- OCR and voice capture;
- broader integrations;
- universal inbox;
- automatic scheduling;
- advanced analytics;
- broad personal knowledge modelling.

This prevents interesting ideas from silently becoming roadmap commitments.

### Stage 2 — Choose one real obligation

Use one existing life project with meaningful consequences, multiple steps, uncertainty, interruption risk, and observable progress. The Disability Tax Credit process is a strong candidate from the master document, but the active roadmap review should confirm the pilot rather than assume it.

### Stage 3 — Write the outcome contract before implementation

The pilot contract must define:

- intended user;
- problem and current failure pattern;
- desired real-world outcome;
- mechanism Kitty claims will help;
- privacy, safety, and avoidance risks;
- required evidence;
- explicit failure conditions;
- when to change or abandon the approach.

Example desired outcome:

> After an interruption, Jacob can open Kitty, understand the truthful project state quickly, and take or deliberately schedule one meaningful next action.

### Stage 4 — Build only the minimum required slices

1. **Truthful project state:** purpose, status, requirements, documents, missing items, blockers, decisions, latest verified action, source dates, and next move.
2. **Deliberate capture:** preserve a fact, task, decision, question, needed document, or risk; inferred obligations require confirmation.
3. **One next move:** concrete, appropriately small, outcome-connected, explainable, editable, and consciously postponable.
4. **Action preparation:** one bounded checklist, draft, request, question set, form location, or call note.
5. **Outcome receipt:** attempted action, actual result, evidence, blocker, and new state.
6. **Resume:** reconstruct the project after a gap without chat history.
7. **Manual graceful degradation:** explicit support mode rather than automated state inference.

### Stage 5 — Measure the mechanism

Primary measures:

- reorientation time after absence;
- accepted next-action initiation;
- percentage of attempts ending in a recorded state;
- re-entry latency after interruption;
- ability to reconstruct state from durable evidence;
- unsupported-claim rate;
- agency failures, with a target of zero;
- interruption outcomes: acted on, postponed, corrected, or repeatedly dismissed.

Qualitative evidence:

- Did Kitty reduce the cost of returning?
- Did preparation make action easier?
- Was the next move small enough?
- Did the user understand why it mattered?
- Did continuity survive without overload?
- Did Kitty advance the obligation or become a substitute activity?

Avoid initially:

- time spent in Kitty;
- message count;
- feature usage;
- streaks;
- raw task volume;
- code shipped per week;
- confidence without accuracy evidence;
- broad wellbeing claims.

### Stage 6 — Require expansion gates

1. Same user, second project.
2. High-value interruption only after trustworthy state exists.
3. Adaptive sizing based on explicit feedback and outcomes.
4. Personal pattern modelling only with consent, visible inputs, correction, deletion, uncertainty, and evidence that simple rules failed.
5. Integrations only when a demonstrated capture or continuity bottleneck requires them.
6. Safety-sensitive health or crisis behaviour only under a separate safety design and external review.

## Meta-controls against strategy failure

### One-loop rule

No new major product lane until one real human loop is completed and evaluated.

### Life-to-machine test

Every Builder or infrastructure task must name the life-facing bottleneck it removes. If it cannot, it remains backlog work.

### Friction-led automation

Do not run broad "what else can AI automate?" exercises as an execution strategy. Capture recurring burdens when they occur and automate only proven repetition.

### Insight maturity

Label insights as observation, interpretation, hypothesis, tested rule, or retired idea. Emotional force is not evidence.

### Activated-state protection

Preserve large plans produced during overtired or highly activated periods, but do not promote them until reviewed after rest against roadmap authority, repository reality, capacity, falsifiable outcomes, and opportunity cost.

### Scope-cost accounting

Every subsystem creates maintenance, security, migration, interface, test, documentation, and archaeology costs. "Could help" is insufficient; it must outperform a smaller intervention.
