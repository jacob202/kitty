## The five user-facing ideas everything converges to

1. **Continue** — resume the right work without reconstructing context.
2. **Ask** — collaborate through chat and direct manipulation.
3. **Build** — delegate durable work through KittyBuilder.
4. **History** — understand what changed, what happened, and why.
5. **Proof** — inspect sources, memory, actions, receipts, uncertainty.

Most capabilities below should become **infrastructure beneath those five ideas**, not separate modes, screens, or brands.

## Experience laws (all future surfaces)

1. One obvious primary action per surface.
2. Progressive disclosure — plain language by default, technical detail one tap away.
3. No dead ends — answers offer a direct continuation (apply, save, build, verify, resume).
4. No fake simplicity — failure, uncertainty, stale state, approval needs stay visible.
5. No configuration tax — Auto works well; manual controls only for meaningful preferences.
6. Power without babysitting — long work runs through durable, bounded systems with progress, recovery, evidence.
7. Governed change only — routing/policy may improve from evidence, never drift silently.
